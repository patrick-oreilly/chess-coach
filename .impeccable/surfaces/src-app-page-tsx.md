## Direction contract

THESIS: Chess Coach is an uncompromising Swiss Brutalist & Topographic Instrument Grid — an edge-to-edge architectural workbench of 1px solid ink cells, oversized tabular KPI readouts, and contour-mapped win-equity ridges that refuses the rounded-card SaaS dashboard template.

OWN-WORLD: Warm survey parchment ground (#f3efe6), raised ivory vellum cells (#fbf9f4), sunken stone headers (#e6dfd0), exposed 1px and 2px structural ink rules (#161c24), copper-terracotta blunder indicators (#c43c21), and alpine survey green (#146c47) for engine refutations and positive equity. Zero border radius (`0px`), zero drop-shadow decoration, zero floating cards. Typography pairs STIX Two Text for display titles with Geist Sans for compact labels and JetBrains/Geist Mono (`tabular-nums`) for all coordinates, evaluations, clocks, and move matrices.

STORY: The player opens the workbench after a session on Chess.com and immediately reads the 5-cell top KPI ticker (active match, accuracy %, blunder/mistake count, live Stockfish evaluation, and one-click "NEXT BLUNDER →" trigger). They step through mistakes on the mineral slate/sandstone chessboard alongside the topographic win-equity ridge, switch to the Drill Deck to solve their own blunders within 0.3 pawns of Stockfish's best line, and inspect the Leak Matrix to see exact Bird's Opening (1.f4) and Pirc/Modern (1...d6) variations and rushed-move impulse rates costing them rating.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed architectural grid at 1440×900: top structural command bar (`1.F4 // CHESS COACH` masthead, numbered mode cells `01 / POST-MORTEM`, `02 / DRILL DECK`, `03 / LEAK MATRIX`, time-control selector, DeepSeek status, Sync trigger); directly below, a 5-column full-bleed KPI ticker strip separated by 1px solid ink rules; below that, the 3-zone Post-Mortem matrix (Zone 01: Archive Index table; Zone 02: Chessground instrument stage with vertical eval gauge, optional side-by-side Engine Comparison split, and 5-cell transport bar; Zone 03: Topographic Win-Equity Ridge with hatched blunder cliffs, diagnostic coach readout, and 2-column tabular move matrix).

FORM: Cartographic Contour & Swiss Brutalist Instrument Grid, rank 5 of 7 grounded directions. seed: none (unavailable)
